Four Steps, No Shortcuts

Every batch is cupped before it leaves the building. If it misses, it does not ship.

01

Sourcing

We buy from seven farms we have visited, at prices agreed a season ahead so growers are not exposed to the spot market.

02

Profiling

Each new lot gets six test roasts across a range of curves. We keep the one that tastes most like the coffee, not most like us.

03

Roasting

Ten kilos at a time on a restored 1962 drum roaster, logged by hand. Two roast days a week, Tuesday and Friday.

04

Resting & Shipping

Beans rest four days to degas, then ship the same day they are bagged. The roast date is stamped on every bag.
