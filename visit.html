Getting Here

Metro

Line 2 to Harbour Lane, exit B. The museum is a three-minute walk north along the water.

Bus

Routes 14, 27 and 60 stop directly outside the entrance. Route 60 runs every ten minutes on weekends.

By Car

Limited paid parking on Mill Street, two blocks east. There is no on-site car park.

By Bicycle

Covered racks sit beside the north wing. A public bike-share dock is at the corner of Harbour Lane and Quay Road.

Good to Know

Can I take photographs?

Yes, without flash or tripod, in all permanent collection galleries. Photography is not permitted in Gallery Two, where loaned works are shown under lender restrictions.

Is the museum accessible?

All galleries are step-free and reachable by lift. Wheelchairs and folding stools can be borrowed free of charge at the cloakroom. Assistance dogs are welcome throughout the building.

Where can I leave bags and coats?

The cloakroom is to the left of the entrance and is free to use. Large backpacks and luggage must be checked in before entering the galleries.

Is there somewhere to eat?

The ground-floor café serves coffee and a short lunch menu until 16:00. The courtyard is open in dry weather and you are welcome to bring your own food there.

Do you offer guided tours?

Free thirty-minute introductions begin at 11:00 and 15:00 from the main hall, no booking needed. Group tours of ten or more should be arranged two weeks in advance.
